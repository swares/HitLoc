How it works

  1. Pick a fightChoose a war's table and a weapon. Each table comes from a real record: skeletons, surgeons' reports, pension rolls, medical studies.
  2. Set the sceneOptional situations (higher ground, fleeing, flanked, cover), a called shot, target armour, and an attack and defence roll.
  3. Roll the hitGet the location (down to the left forearm), wound type and severity, and its effects: bleeding, pain, impairment, time to death untreated.
  4. Track the woundedApply hits to casualties, bleed them by round or minute, bind and tourniquet, and let their wounds cut their attack and defence.
